# ADR-NNNN: Short title of the decision

- **Status:** proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** names or handles of the maintainers who decide
- **Related:** links to RFCs, issues, Discussions or other ADRs

## Context

What is the problem? What forces are at play: technical, legal, organisational? What constraints apply? Write this so that someone who joins the project in two years understands why a decision was needed.

## Considered options

1. Option one.
2. Option two.
3. Option three.

## Decision

The option we chose, stated in one or two sentences, followed by the reasons.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, and what becomes harder, because of this decision. Include the negative consequences. List any follow-up work.

### Sovereignty impact

How this decision affects each commitment in [`SOVEREIGNTY.md`](../../SOVEREIGNTY.md). Write "no impact" where that is the case.

## More information

Links, references, and notes on how to check that the decision is followed.
