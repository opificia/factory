# Trademark policy

This policy explains how you may use the Opificia AI name and logos. It is adapted from the trademark policies of the Linux Foundation and the OpenInfra Foundation. It is not a copy of either, and they have not reviewed or endorsed it.

## Why we have a trademark policy

The code is open source, and you may use, change and share it under its licence. The name and logos are different: they tell people that something comes from the Opificia project. If anyone could put the name on anything, people could no longer trust it. This policy protects that trust, without getting in the way of normal use.

"Opificia", "Opificia AI" and the Opificia AI logos are trademarks of Experience Lab BV. They are not registered yet, so we use the ™ symbol, not the symbol for a registered trademark. The steward holds them for the project and intends to transfer them to a neutral foundation if the project moves to one (see [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md)).

## The licence does not grant trademark rights

The AGPL-3.0, Apache-2.0, CC-BY-4.0 and CC0-1.0 licences in this repository cover copyright. None of them grants any right to use the Opificia name or logos. The files in `brand/` are not open source; [`LICENSES/LicenseRef-Opificia-Trademark.txt`](LICENSES/LicenseRef-Opificia-Trademark.txt) applies to them.

## Uses that are allowed without asking

You may, without asking us:

- **Refer to the project** by name, in articles, talks, books, courses, comparisons and reviews, as long as you do not suggest the project endorses you.
- **State compatibility**: "compatible with Opificia", "for Opificia", "built on Opificia", "an Opificia plugin". The statement must be true.
- **Distribute unmodified copies** of official Opificia releases or source under the Opificia name.
- **Use the logo to link to the project**, for example on a website listing tools you use, or on slides about Opificia.
- **Run community events and groups** about Opificia, such as meetups or user groups, if the name makes clear they are not run by the project (for example, "Opificia meetup Ghent"). Tell us about them; we would like to know.

## Uses that need permission

Ask us first if you want to:

- **Offer a hosted or managed service under the name**, such as "Opificia Cloud", "Opificia Hosted" or "Managed Opificia". You may always say that your service is "built on Opificia" or "runs Opificia".
- **Distribute a modified version under the name.** If you change the code, give your version a different name. You may say it is "based on Opificia".
- **Use the name or logo in a company, product, domain or social media account name.**
- **Sell merchandise** with the name or logo.
- **Use the logo in a way not described here.**

We aim to answer requests within 15 working days. Contact: Experience Lab BV, at <hi@opificia.ai>.

## Uses that are never allowed

- Suggesting that the project or the steward endorses, certifies or supports your product or service when it does not.
- Using the name or logo for software that is not Opificia, or that misleads people about its origin.
- Using a name or logo confusingly similar to Opificia's.

## Logo usage rules

These rules come from the Opificia AI brand book. [`brand/README.md`](brand/README.md) explains which file to use where.

- **Use only the files in [`brand/`](brand/).** Never redraw, recolour, rotate, stretch or retype them.
- **The gold tooth always sits at one o'clock.** Do not rotate the mark.
- **Never make several or all twelve teeth gold.** Twelve gold marks in a circle on blue reads as the EU emblem, and Opificia is not an EU institution.
- Keep a clear space of one quarter of the mark's height around the logo.
- Use the logo at no less than 120px wide (lockup) or 24px (mark). Below 24px, use `opificia-favicon.svg`.
- Inside the logo the name is lowercase, `opificia`. In text, write **Opificia AI** on first mention and **Opificia** after that.

## Reporting misuse

If you see the name or logo used in a way that breaks this policy, tell us at <hi@opificia.ai>.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may change through the process in [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md). Uses that were allowed when you started remain allowed under the version you relied on, unless they mislead people.
