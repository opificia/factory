# Getting help

This page explains where to ask questions about Opificia AI.

## Ask in the open

Ask questions in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/opificia/factory/discussions), in the **Q&A** category. Answers there help the next person with the same question.

- **Ideas and feature requests**: the **Ideas** category of Discussions.
- **Bugs**: open an issue with the bug report form. There is no product code yet, so bugs will mostly be in documentation and repository settings for now.
- **Vulnerabilities**: never in public. Follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
- **Code of conduct concerns**: email <conduct@opificia.ai>. See [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## What the project does not offer

The maintainers do not give private support by email or direct message. This is not unfriendliness: answering in public means everyone benefits, and it keeps the work spread fairly across volunteers. Please do not contact maintainers directly with support questions.

We cannot promise response times in Discussions. [`docs/community/triage.md`](docs/community/triage.md) describes the goals we aim for.

## Commercial support

Commercial support may be offered by Experience Lab BV or by other companies. Details will follow here. The project does not endorse any support provider.

For other questions about the project, email <hi@opificia.ai>.
